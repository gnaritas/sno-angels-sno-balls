The difference

Sno ball, not snow cone

A snow cone is crushed ice and syrup that mostly sits on top. A New Orleans sno ball is shaved so fine it holds the flavor all the way through.

Why it matters

Soft snow drinks up the syrup

Unlike a crunchy snow cone, a New Orleans sno ball starts with ice shaved so fine it’s soft and fluffy as fresh snow — so the flavored syrup soaks all the way through instead of pooling at the bottom. Every spoonful tastes the same as the first, right down to the last bite of ice.
