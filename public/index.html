How to order

Build your mound in three steps

  1. 1

    Shaved like snow

    The ice runs through the machine so fine it falls soft and fluffy, like fresh snow — so the syrup soaks all the way through instead of pooling at the bottom like a snow cone.

  2. 2

    Up to three flavors

    Pick your mix from the boards — more than 50 flavors, including kosher, dye-free and sugar-free choices, plus made-to-order fresh fruit.

  3. 3

    Stuff it, float it

    Push ice cream inside the mound, pour sweet cream over the top, and pile on the add-ins at the counter.

More than snowballs

A shelf for the neighborhood

Inside the shop, the shelves carry cookies, jewelry, hats and other goods from other local businesses. Owner David Marquez wanted the space to help Tomball makers grow — so a line for a sno ball doubles as a browse through somebody else’s bakery or jewelry bench.

The family behind it

Service first, then a snowball shop

Sno Angels is a family-owned, veteran-owned shop. David Marquez served in the Air Force and then spent his later career as a federal firefighter working with NASA, before retiring and building this corner shop with his family.

The old insurance-office building on Main and Magnolia came back to life as part of the city’s Old Town facade program — blanketed in pink, white and green paint, with handmade artwork and new signage. It is the family’s second Tomball business; the Agape Salon is theirs too.
