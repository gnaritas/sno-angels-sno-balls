One mound, up to three flavors

More than 50 flavors line the boards, and a sno ball is yours for the mixing — pick up to three and the syrup goes through the whole mound, not just over it. Kosher, dye-free and sugar-free choices are on the board too, and fresh fruit gets made to order.
